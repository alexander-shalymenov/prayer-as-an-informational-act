Anti-demonstration: exact prayer and added noise

This section shows why the informational act must remain structurally exact. Additional conditions, contradiction, or vague intention increase cost and make the desired outcome harder to actualize.

Exact prayer (perfect resonance)

“I request a promotion to senior position. I have completed all projects, acquired skills. No other requests.”
Activates exactly Foundation G. Algorithmic Purity. Target Configuration. No extra nodes, no contradictions.
Desired (Promotion) K=0.6  p=0.4  F=0.20
Default (No promotion) K=1  p=0.6  F=0.40
Prayer succeeds: Promotion actualizes
Lower F wins. Only exact prayer makes desired F smaller.

Extra wish (added condition)

“Grant me promotion, and also give me a company car and a parking spot.”
Adds extra conditions. K and p increase for all outcomes. Desired cost rises above no promotion.
Desired (Promotion) K=1.1  p=0.7  F=0.40
Default (No promotion) K=0.9  p=0.7  F=0.20
Prayer fails: No promotion
Lower F wins. Only exact prayer makes desired F smaller.

Contradiction / vague

“I want a promotion, but I’m not sure I deserve it. Maybe a small raise is fine too.”
Missing Algorithmic Purity. Contradiction increases p(Ω). Desired outcome becomes too expensive.
Desired (Promotion) K=1.2  p=0.9  F=0.30
Default (No promotion) K=0.9  p=0.7  F=0.20
Prayer fails: No promotion
Lower F wins. Only exact prayer makes desired F smaller.
